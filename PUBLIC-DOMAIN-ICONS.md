# Daheer public-domain vector assets

The upgraded vector decorations and icon artwork authored for Daheer are released under **CC0 1.0 Universal**, dedicating them to the public domain to the extent permitted by law.

Included originals:
- `src/assets/vectors/eight-point-spark-cc0.svg`
- `src/assets/vectors/botanical-frame-cc0.svg`
- The refreshed inline UI symbol paths in `src/index.html`

The decorative spark is an eight-ray star made from four long and four short tapered blades with a concave center, tilted 12 degrees. It is deliberately not built from a plus sign, cross glyph, or rotated cross.

Third-party files remain under their own licenses; this notice does not re-license third-party artwork, fonts, or audio.


### Four-ray decorative star
`src/assets/vectors/eight-point-spark-cc0.svg` is an original CC0 1.0 vector with eight tapered rays (four long, four short) and a concave center. It is used as a mask in CSS so the decorative silhouette cannot render as a plus/cross glyph.
