# Daheer Adventure

Adventure is a 30-day skill-learning challenge. On first use, the user enters an estimate of how many hours per day they spend on habits they consider unhelpful.

## How the roller works

The estimate is not used as a score. It sets the amount of practice time that can realistically be reclaimed. Adventure then builds a candidate list from the skill catalog by requiring each skill's recommended daily practice time to fit that estimate. A small weighted-random layer nudges the roll toward skills with a stronger ability to replace lost time, while the final selection remains random.

The candidate catalog currently includes:

- Morse code — 30 min/day — 15 h/month
- Typing — 20 min/day — 10 h/month
- Drawing nature — 30 min/day — 15 h/month
- Public speaking — 25 min/day — 12.5 h/month
- Chess — 45 min/day — 22.5 h/month
- Cooking — 45 min/day — 22.5 h/month
- French — 60 min/day — 30 h/month
- Spanish — 60 min/day — 30 h/month
- Wrestling — 60 min/day — 30 h/month
- Programming — 90 min/day — 45 h/month

A selected skill becomes a normal daily Daheer habit and is locked as the Adventure focus for exactly 30 days. The selected skill's goal, daily practice time, and monthly practice target are stored with the habit and plan.

Guitar is intentionally not part of the Adventure catalog or the habit emoji suggestions.
