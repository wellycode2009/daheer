# Daheer asset licensing ledger

## Icons
The interface icons are **Tabler Icons** (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2020-2026 Paweł Kuna), copied into the icon sprite in `src/index.html`. Brand (company logo) icons from Tabler are not used. The full MIT text is in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.

## Original Daheer vectors
The botanical frame and eight-point spark in `src/assets/vectors/` are original Daheer artwork released under **CC0 1.0 Universal**.

## Ambient sounds (BG sounds)
- The seven built-in sounds (cafe, stream, wind, wave, rain, fireplace, birds) are **synthesized in the app** with the Web Audio API. No audio files ship with Daheer.
- **Blanket** (github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket, GPL-3.0-or-later) was studied only for its design ideas (a simple mixer of looping sounds with a volume control). **No Blanket code, sound files or assets are used.** The earlier Blanket `.ogg` files were removed because their licenses differ file by file (some are CC-BY and need attribution).

## Your own sounds
Users can add their own audio files with the plus button. Those files stay in that user's own saved data. Daheer does not include or redistribute them.

## Fonts and branding
Bundled Latin fonts, Thmanyah fonts, and Daheer brand assets are not re-licensed by this file. Preserve their source licenses/permissions when redistributing.
